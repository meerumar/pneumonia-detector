How it works

  1. Upload - your X-ray is sent to a FastAPI server.
  2. Preprocess - resized to 224x224 and normalized.
  3. Predict - a ResNet18 deep learning model outputs probabilities for NORMAL and PNEUMONIA.
  4. Explain - Grad-CAM draws a heatmap of the regions that drove the decision.
